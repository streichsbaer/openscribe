Parakeet speech models

OpenScribe downloads these models on demand from Hugging Face at pinned revisions and does not modify them.

Parakeet Ultra
  Files: FluidInference/parakeet-ultra-coreml, a Core ML conversion of moondream/parakeet-ultra,
  which is a post-trained version of NVIDIA parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3.
  https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/parakeet-ultra-coreml
  https://huggingface.co/moondream/parakeet-ultra
  https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3
